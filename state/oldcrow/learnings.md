# What works on Old Crow (the bot keeps this up to date from Buffer insights)

Last updated: 2026-10-25 (Sunday review; per-post metrics refreshed 2026-10-24; Buffer totals: 64k impressions last 7 days vs 418k the week before)
Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares) / impressions (Buffer's own rate also counts clicks, so it reads higher).

## Do more
- One-liner posters (old raven, cream poster): still the best type overall (big reach posts are mostly one-liners: 49k, 18.3k, 13.7k).
- Tech / online irony one-liners with a hard twist: Self-checkout 1.54%, Buy now pay later 1.53%, Everyone's an expert online 2.37%, A thousand friends online 1.82% (13 shares, 4 comments).
- Set-up then twist about advice, work or modern habits ("Everyone wants honest advice" 1.33%, 49 shares; "Work hard, they said" 1.39%, 49 shares, 7 comments).
- Then vs Now on community themes: Front porch. Front camera. 1.76%, Who borrows sugar 0.84%; non-product themes avg ~0.9% over 6 vs 0.81% for made-to-last.
- Strongest line of the day in the evening slot (the big-reach posts mostly went out 19:30-21:00).

## Do less
- Soft "shared everyday moment" one-liners (snooze, early night, coffee sometime, long goodbyes): ~0.8% avg over 4, no comments.
- Then vs Now "made to last / repairable": huge reach but low rate (0.65%, 0.62%, 0.49%).
- Pure money-math one-liners (Free shipping 0.97%, They sold you the dream 1.06%).

## Test next
- Then vs Now on advertising, houses and school themes (advertising tested 2026-10-26).
- One-liners about technology getting in the way (updates, passwords, smart gadgets) vs about people.
- One-liner caption length: 1-3 words vs 4-6 words.
- Posting hours: need 30+ new-style posts at 3 a day before trusting time patterns.
