# What works on Old Crow (the bot keeps this up to date from Buffer insights)

Last updated: 2026-10-18 (33 posts measured; Buffer metrics refreshed 2026-10-18)
Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares) / impressions (Buffer's own rate also counts clicks, so it reads higher).

## Do more
- One-liner posters (old raven, cream poster): best type since they started. 1.35% avg over 8 posts, 110 shares, and 3 of the 4 biggest posts (18.3k, 10.9k, 3.0k impressions).
- Lines with a set-up then a twist about advice, work or modern habits ("Everyone wants honest advice. Until they get it." 1.33%, 49 shares; "Work hard, they said. Your reward: more work." 1.50%, 42 shares, 7 comments; "Everyone wants a quiet life. Until the wifi goes down." 1.64%).
- Evening slot for new-style posts: 1.15% avg and ~11.3k avg impressions over 5 posts vs ~2.3k-2.9k for morning/midday.
- Single-image AI posters (Luke's rule); keep the old raven large and the text big.

## Do less
- Then vs Now with the "made to last / repairable" theme: huge reach (25k, 8.3k) but low rate (0.65%, 0.61%); 0.81% avg over 5 then-vs-now posts. Keep rotating to new themes (Luke's variety rule).
- Pure money-math one-liners land lower ("Free shipping..." 0.97%, "They sold you the dream..." 1.06%) than people/habit lines, except "You saved fifty dollars..." (2.0%, small reach).
- Price, question and told-or-sold posts are gone (Luke's rule); question averaged 0.28%-0.83%.

## Test next
- Then vs Now on non-product themes (photos, time, community, food) to see if the rate rises above ~0.8%.
- Comments are still rare (max 7): try one-liners about shared everyday moments people want to reply to (no engagement bait).
- One-liner caption length: 1-3 words vs 4-6 words.
- Posting hours: need 30+ new-style posts before trusting time patterns.
